ATS, or Applicant Tracking System, is like a robot that helps companies find the right people for a job. It looks at resumes and picks out the ones that match what the company needs, so they don’t have to look through all the resumes themselves.
An ATS-optimized CV is a resume that is specially designed to be easily read by a computer system called an Applicant Tracking System (ATS). This system helps companies sort through job applications by looking for specific words and formats. An ATS-optimized CV makes sure your resume gets noticed by both the computer and the person hiring.
